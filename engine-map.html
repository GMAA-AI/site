The GMAA Engine Map

The engine's own map. From v2.7.2 the map ships inside the engine as ENGINE-MAP.md, checked against the zip both ways at the release gate. This page renders that file as written, so what you read here is what your download carries. Engine package v2.7.2 · canon pin v1.6.9 · cite by version. Verification fingerprints are on gmaa.ai/versions.html.

What this is. The inventory of the engine, spec to code: ten areas, every file with its role, each anchored to the canon section it implements. It describes engine v2.7.2 at canon pin v1.6.9 (GOVERNED_MULTI_AGENT_ARCHITECTURE_v1_6_9.md); PROVENANCE.tsv carries each file's origin and hash. Read this before you look for anything; if a file is not listed here, it is not part of the engine.

Core read. The files marked Core read, every seat are read end to end by every seat before its first act in the project; those marked Core read, by role are read whole by the seats CLAUDE.md names under its Core read section, which also gives the receipt line (CORE-READ:) each seat prints when the read is done.

The shape of the whole thing in one paragraph. The canon states one correction: authorize the set, not the change, with an integrator seat assessing and an independent human ratifying. The engine is that correction made mechanical, in layers that check one another: identity derived from location and halted on mismatch (area 2); a harness plane that denies before any agent decides (area 3); a set ledger, an assembler and a ratification line (area 4); a work lifecycle with no edge from in-progress to closed except an audited receipt (area 5); merge-time backstops (area 6); transport that lives outside every participant (area 7); and an audit layer whose invariants are probed, not asserted, with a governance index that makes "cite before raise" a command (area 8). Areas 9 and 10 are how an adopter gets in and how a recipient verifies what it got.

2 Identity, seat lifecycle, and the restart loop

Canon anchors: §1.0 definitions, §1.1 seats and surfaces, §2 one session one seat, §7 the seat manifest and governed lifecycle (§7.1 Boot-0, §7.2 provisioning, §7.3 project-qualified identity, §7.4 liveness, §7.5 the line every seat carries). Contract: contracts/LAUNCH-CONTRACT.md.

The design law of this area: identity comes from location, is derived on both sides of the spawn boundary, and the two derivations must agree or the seat halts. New at v2.7 against v2.6: the seat ends itself and is relaunched by a loop its tmux session runs, with hooks at session start, prompt, compaction and stop that record and enforce the boundary; the operator's stop verb is the only thing that breaks the loop.

3 The harness plane and single-writer enforcement

Canon anchors: §1.1 single-writer invariant, §1.2 the two-plane authorization model, §1.3 subagents, §6 transport identity (seat keys), §8 permission architecture. Contract: contracts/MECH-WRITE.md.

This area answers one question mechanically: can anyone but foundation land bytes on the spine, and can any session do what its seat may not? The answer is layered: a harness hook denies before the model acts, a commit hook denies at the commit, a push hook denies at the push, and a key per seat signs what passes. The server-side twin of the push hook is held, not shipped: the contracts name it as a check an adopter supplies on a forge the seats do not administer, and no shipped file listens on a workstation.

The loose release layer, beside the zip (not zip members; listed here so the envelope is whole): README.md the front door and the map of the release; README.rtf its rich-text rendering; LICENSE.md the licensing envelope, engine under Apache 2.0 with the Commons Clause, the canon file carved out under CC BY 4.0; copies of MANIFEST.sha256 and PROVENANCE.tsv; and SHA256SUMS, seven rows (the engine zip, the decision kit zip, the two copies, the README pair and the license), the first verify surface, checked before anything is unzipped. The zip carries no license or README member of its own: the license reaches a zip-only recipient through the cover's pointer and the release page, which is why the loose layer travels with the zip.

Execution wiring, the whole engine as a timeline

At adoption: SHA256SUMS verifies the downloads; the decision kit's paste (00-PASTE-THIS.txt) has a chat session read WHY-GMAA.md, the canon and the cover and say adopt or do not adopt; the operator decides; the same session, with the Phase 4 files attached and the canon and this map read end to end, settles the nine decisions and returns docs/FOUNDATION.md, seats.yaml, the coordination sections, the subagent definitions, the charters and genesis.conf; then, in the unzipped package, bash genesis.sh verifies MANIFEST.sha256 at the fresh unzip and runs fill, check, seed, enroll, hooks and the launch in order (MANIFEST.sha256 verified at the fresh unzip, the two Phase 4 files skipped; scripts/install_pre_commit_hook.sh refuses until every writing lane is confirmed), and foundation, after its core read, lands each other roster seat with genesis.sh lane; the adopter's deny classes and substrate rows go into their tables as its first governed work.

At seat launch: the operator runs launch-orc.sh in the worktree; it resolves the stamp through scripts/resolve-lane.sh and scripts/resolve-project.sh, refuses double-launch, and creates {project}-{lane} running scripts/seat-loop.sh; the session loads .claude/agents/orc.md; the session-start hooks fire scripts/boot-check.sh, scripts/seat-boot-record.sh and the subagent ledger; the seat derives identity, reads its charter and dispatch/LATEST.md, then its inbox.

Per prompt and per tool call: the prompt hooks record observed mode and read the exit cue; on every Bash call the greenlight policy, the background deny and the subagent commit deny run; on every tool the subagent cap runs.

Per change: foundation dispatches into an inbox and rings scripts/doorbell.sh; the lane works on its branch; the reconciliation diff is run by hand when arrival is in question (no hook runs it); the state machine admits no closure without an audited receipt.

Per commit: .git/hooks/pre-commit reads the declared spine paths at HEAD and blocks non-writer spine writes and uncovered foundation writes; the commit-msg hook runs the creed scan; the pre-push hook verifies the seat's signature; .git/hooks/post-commit reaps, pushes and launches the auditor, whose code half runs every time.

Per set: members enter the ledger via scripts/pending-set.sh; ratify.sh assemble computes joint edges against the substrate table and writes the packet; assess records foundation's reasoning; the cost brake is run by hand before each metered call (cost-guard.sh check; the assembler does not call it); acts in the build class pass scripts/build-class-check.sh without a pen; everything else waits for the operator's one decision line, which ratify.sh ratify records; foundation merges, scripts/reap_at_merge.sh backstops.

At every seat boundary: the seat runs scripts/seat-exit.sh as its last act, which writes the intent; when the turn ends the stop hook runs the creed scan on the spine's bytes, writes the audit seal through scripts/audit-seal.sh, reads the hub, and on the fresh intent sends the exit; the loop relaunches the seat, unless the intent was shutdown or the operator's scripts/seat-stop.sh said otherwise.

Continuously: the seven standing probes run on their schedules by tier, ledgered; the budget probe holds the hot files under their caps; the index probe holds the governance index to its corpus.

Coverage account

The engine zip is 189 files, every one a row above, and 6 loose files beside it (README.md, README.rtf, LICENSE.md, SHA256SUMS and the two zips; gmaa-decide_v2.7.2.zip is four of the zip's members copied flat). By area: area 1 carries 3; area 2 carries 33; area 3 carries 25; area 4 carries 20; area 5 carries 10; area 6 carries 7; area 7 carries 21; area 8 carries 41; area 9 carries 24; area 10 carries 5; total 189. Every zip path appears exactly once across the tables and every table path exists in the zip; the release gate re-runs that check at every candidate and fails on one missing, extra or duplicate row.

By provenance, PROVENANCE.tsv's own kinds: 150 origin files byte-equal to the upstream pin (the upstream's whole functional closure: the wired scripts, hooks and probes, and its contracts, indexes and documents), 1 derived index (the governance index, regenerated by the shipped generator, one frame line differing from the pin's blob), 1 public canon, 35 package-authored members (the adoption documents and their renderings, the skeletons, genesis.sh, and this map), plus the manifest and the provenance table: 189.

Two absences are load-bearing and named: seats.yaml does not ship (the adopter's roster, written in Phase 4 from the skeleton; genesis.sh reads it and derives the registry from it, and the boot check reads it as the genesis marker from the moment it exists), and no update classifier ships (the upgrade runbook is manual).
