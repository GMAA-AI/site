How versions work

Every version is cited on two axes together: the package version (the engine artifact) and the canon pin (the specification version the artifact ships and conforms to). Every content change is a new version; nothing is ever rebuilt in place under an existing label. Verify every download against its fingerprints before use.

How it ships

Two layers, so you can read before you download and verify what you run.

Readable at the repository root, no download. The README, the adoption cover, the getting-started walkthrough, the two operator's guides, the specification (canon/), the license, the changelog, and the citation file all sit at the root of the repository. You can read the argument, work through the adoption flow, and read or cite the specification without downloading anything.

One sealed download, the engine. The machinery you run ships as a single sealed zip: the governance scripts, agent definitions, contracts, schema, and operating docs, with its own MANIFEST.sha256. You download that one artifact, verify it, and unzip it into your project folder.

The split follows the licensing. The specification is free under CC BY 4.0 and is the cite-by-version target, so it stays readable and citeable at the root without touching the restricted download. The engine is source-available under Apache 2.0 with the Commons Clause, which is not an OSI open-source license: free to use, modify, and redistribute, but not to sell or offer as a paid service. It is the one controlled artifact.

Loose at the root does not mean absent from your project. During instantiation you place the specification and the reader docs into your project folder alongside the unzipped engine, and genesis reads them there. The engine zip does not contain the documents, and the documents are not sealed inside it.

The first public release of the GMAA engine: a faithful generic extraction of a live production multi-agent architecture, independently reviewed pre-release by the production program it derives from.

In the box: - The complete governance machinery: lane resolution, launch, doorbell and inbox transport, sync, validation, audit hooks, standing probes, and the reaper, with per-file integrity (MANIFEST.sha256). - Seven agent definitions, including the orchestrator, the dispatch executor, and the optional code-substrate architect seat. - The portable contracts and schema slots, the operating contract, the standing disciplines with boot-creed trigger binding, and the project-foundation skeleton your architect instantiates. - The specification (canon v1.5) at canon/, also published at gmaa.ai/canon.html under CC BY 4.0. - First-launch genesis of all runtime-born state surfaces, a fresh installation boots clean, with every guard-checked file created and stamped at first launch. - Program-qualified session identity per spec §7.3 ({program}-{lane}, stamped at provisioning): multiple GMAA programs co-reside on one machine without collision, and the doorbell resolves exactly one target or halts. - scripts/preflight.sh, the substrate floor (Claude Code CLI, git with GitHub access, tmux; WSL2 on Windows) made testable: pass/fail per item, loud on any miss.

Known behavior on a fresh installation: the orchestration-freshness probe reads STALE until your program's first decision binds, it measures orchestration life, and a newborn installation has none yet. It self-clears at the first bound decision; it is a report, not a halt.

Companion artifacts (alongside the archive, not inside it): - The Operator's Guide, the human-side manual: the ferry, the doorbell, shutdown handshakes, restarts, and what to do when a seat halts.

Verify your download

Confirm the bytes are the bytes, the same discipline the engine itself teaches. The repository root publishes a SHA256SUMS file listing the sha256 of the sealed engine zip and of the specification.

Verify the engine you run:

sha256sum gmaa-engine_v2.5.3.zip # compare to SHA256SUMS unzip gmaa-engine_v2.5.3.zip -d gmaa-engine cd gmaa-engine sha256sum -c MANIFEST.sha256 # every line OK, or stop and report

Confirm which specification version you have:

sha256sum canon/GOVERNED_MULTI_AGENT_ARCHITECTURE_v1_6.pdf # compare to SHA256SUMS

The canon sha in SHA256SUMS is the value the engine pins, so a match proves the loose specification at the root is the exact version this engine conforms to.

The specification

The specification is versioned separately from the engine. Current: v1.6 (2026-08-11), published at canon.html and available as a PDF at canon/GOVERNED_MULTI_AGENT_ARCHITECTURE_v1_6.pdf, both under CC BY 4.0. The sealed engine v2.5.3 pins canon v1.5; the next engine seal re-pins to v1.6. Each engine version states which spec it pins, and the canon sha is published in SHA256SUMS so you can confirm the version you hold.
